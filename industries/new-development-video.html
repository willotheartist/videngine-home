New development video: a video for every house type and plot

New development video for housebuilders: a narrated, captioned video for every house type and plot, built from your plot list, CGIs and brochure text.

The short answer

A new development video set gives a housebuilder one overview of the development, a video for each house type and one for each available plot, plus videos for phase launches, the show home and sold-out updates. Each comes from material you already hold: brochure text, house type specifications, the plot list, photos and CGIs. Videngine turns each plot list row into a narrated, captioned 9:16 and 16:9 video, carries your CGI labels, and re-renders a plot's video when its price changes.

The video set for a new development

A development needs a set of videos with different lifespans: an overview that lasts as long as the site, one per house type, and one per plot that lasts only while the plot is available.

What a single property video shows, and the listing-to-video workflow, are in the real estate listing video guide. This page covers what a sales and marketing team runs around it.

Not every plot needs its own video. Make one where plots of a house type differ by price, orientation or specification; identical plots can share the house type video. For build to rent, see rental listing video.

CGIs, show homes and photos in off-plan property video

Use only images you have the right to use in video, label each computer-generated image (CGI) the way your brochure does, and never let a CGI pass as a photograph.

  • Check the licence. CGIs, floor plan artwork and show home photography are usually licensed from a visualiser or photographer. Check the licence covers video, social media and paid ads before the batch runs. Never use another scheme's images, or stock imagery presented as your site.
  • Carry your label on screen. If your marketing labels CGIs, for example “computer-generated image, indicative only”, every CGI frame in both cuts carries the same words. Set it once in the scene set.
  • A CGI is not a photo. The narration never calls a CGI a photo or “your new home”. Keep CGIs and photographs in separate scenes. CGIs often show mature planting, so describe the house type, not the street on completion day.
  • Off-plan first, then built. An off-plan property video uses CGIs and floor plans. Once the plot is built and photographed, swap in its own photos and re-render.

Show homes, upgrades and “from” prices

A show home video should name the house type and say which features are optional. In the UK, CAP's advice on new build and off-plan homes says that where there is no image of the home at the “from” price, an image showing optional upgrades is acceptable only if the price is clearly prefixed “from” and qualifying text, such as “image includes optional upgrades at additional cost”, sits next to the image and is as prominent as the price, for example “from £90,000, home shown £95,000”. In a video, put that text on the same frame as the image and price, not only in the narration.

For registered developers, clause 1.1 of version 2 of the New Homes Quality Code, which applies to homes reserved from 2 March 2026, says sales and marketing material, including photography, CGI and video, must not mislead. Check your obligations with your compliance lead.

Prices and availability by plot

Put prices and availability only in videos you re-render when they change, which means the plot videos, and keep them out of the overview.

  • The overview states what lasts. Not “only six homes left”, and not a “from” price that moves each time the cheapest plot sells. Send viewers to the development page for availability.
  • A price change means a re-render. Update the plot's row and render again. The rebuild is saved as _v2 beside the original; re-rendering the same video in the same month is free. Replace the old cut wherever it was published.
  • Retire reserved and sold plots. Agree one rule, such as taking the video down at reservation, and apply it on your website, YouTube and social accounts. If a reservation falls through, re-release and re-render.
  • Incentives end. Re-render the plot video when an incentive ends, or keep incentives in a separate, dated ad; see video ads from your own content.
  • Scarcity must be true. A “last plots in this phase” line goes out only when the plot list says so that day. Clause 1.3 of the New Homes Quality Code rules out suggesting that other people are interested, or that the price will soon rise, when this is not true. Add “selling fast” to the banned-phrases list.

Many plots of one house type: making each plot video distinct

Every plot video of one house type should be told apart in the first few seconds, so lead with what differs: plot number, its own price, orientation and any upgrades the plot list states.

Plots of one house type share CGIs and floor plans, so without plot-level facts a buyer who watched plot 14 cannot tell it from plot 15.

Questions housebuilders ask about new development video

What should a new development video include?

A set covers the development, each house type and each available plot. The overview shows location, setting and house types, without prices or availability. House type videos show layout and standard specification. Plot videos add the plot number, its own price, orientation and any listed upgrades.

Can I use CGIs in a new homes marketing video?

Yes, if your licence covers video. Label each CGI the way your brochure does, keep the label on screen while it shows, and never describe a CGI as a photo. If an image shows optional upgrades beside a from price, CAP's advice asks for qualifying text next to the image.

How do I make a video for every plot on a development?

Export the plot list as a CSV with one row per released plot, attach each house type's images and run a batch. Each row becomes a narrated, captioned plot video in 9:16 and 16:9, reviewed by the sales team before it goes out.

What happens to a plot video when the price changes or the plot is reserved?

Re-render it from the updated plot list, or retire it. The rebuild is saved as _v2 and replaces the old cut wherever it was published. A reserved or sold plot's video comes down; if the reservation falls through, re-render.

Can Videngine make an architectural flythrough of an unbuilt development?

No. Videngine does not generate 3D or footage; it narrates the CGIs, floor plans and photos you supply with slow pan and zoom. For a flythrough, use your architectural visualisation studio.

The short answer

The best AI video generator is the one built for the job you need done. For new cinematic footage, look at Google Veo, Runway or Kling. For presenter videos, Synthesia or HeyGen. For creator video, InVideo or Fliki; for articles, Pictory or Lumen5; for design-led editing, Canva or Adobe Express; for developer APIs, Shotstack or Creatomate. Generated cinematic footage: Google Veo, Runway and Kling

Choose a footage model when the missing ingredient is a shot that was never filmed.

Google Veo

Google DeepMind's Veo page describes Veo 3.1 as its leading video generation model. It takes text prompts, reference images and first and last frames, and generates audio natively, with outputs in 1080p and 4K. You can try it in the Gemini app and Google Flow.

Runway

Runway's product page lists text to video and image to video, with Gen-4.5 as its frontier video model. Aleph 2.0 edits one frame and carries the change through the clip, and it plugs into Premiere Pro and After Effects.

Kling

Kling's Video 3.0 page lists text to video and image to video, up to 15 seconds in a single generation, with multi-shot sequences, native audio and character-level lip-sync.

OpenAI Sora is not listed: OpenAI's help centre says the Sora web and app were discontinued on 26 April 2026 and the API on 24 September 2026.

A generated shot of a product is an illustration, not evidence of how the real product behaves.

Presenter and avatar videos: Synthesia and HeyGen

Choose an avatar tool when the video needs a person on screen and filming one is impractical.

Synthesia

Synthesia's homepage describes AI avatars and voiceovers in 140+ languages, starting from a script, a document, a URL or a PowerPoint deck. It lists hundreds of avatars, dubbing, approval workflows and a free Basic plan with 500 credits a month (up to 10 minutes of video). See our Synthesia comparison.

HeyGen

HeyGen's homepage leads with videos starring your own Personal Avatar. It takes text, image or audio, turns slides and PDFs into avatar scenes, and translates into 175+ languages and dialects with lip-sync. Custom avatars require on-camera identity verification. It states a free plan that supports text-to-video.

Check consent for any real person's likeness and who reviews translated narration.

Prompt and script to video for creators: InVideo and Fliki

The best AI video creator for social and YouTube work takes an idea, a script or your own clips and leaves you in control of the result.

InVideo

InVideo's homepage calls it an agentic video editor: upload footage, describe the edit in plain words, and its agent works on a multitrack timeline. It gives access to models including Veo 3.1 and Kling. Plans are paid (Starter, Plus, Max), each with a stock footage quota; see current pricing and our InVideo comparison.

Fliki

Fliki's homepage turns a script, a blog post or a one-line prompt into a video with AI voiceover, visuals, music and subtitles; slides become one narrated scene each. It lists 2,000+ voices in 80+ languages and dialects, avatars and a free forever plan.

Our text to video AI guide separates prompt generators from script to video.

Articles into videos with stock media: Pictory and Lumen5

Choose a repurposing tool when the source is writing you already publish.

Pictory

Pictory's homepage turns existing content into edited, captioned videos from scripts, blog posts, URLs, PowerPoint files, audio, images or recordings. Visuals come from stock libraries, its AI assets or your media; narration uses ElevenLabs voices. It says you can get started free, and offers an enterprise API. See our Pictory comparison.

Lumen5

Lumen5's blog-to-video page takes a blog URL, a PDF, typed text or an outline, extracts the key points and pairs the script with media, timing and motion. It lists more than 40 AI voices and invites you to sign up for free.

Check that the summary keeps the article's meaning and that each clip matches the sentence beneath it.

Design-led editing with AI help: Canva and Adobe Express

Choose a design editor when a person builds each video by hand and AI speeds that up.

Canva

Canva's video editor page describes a browser timeline editor with templates for Reels, Shorts and business. AI features include a built-in AI video generator, Magic Video, one-click captions and background removal; Image to Video and Magic Resize are Pro. It exports MP4 or GIF, without a watermark on free accounts that use only free elements. See our Canva comparison.

Adobe Express

Adobe Express's video maker page offers free templates, trimming, resizing per platform, collaboration, captions and royalty-free music. It says anyone can make videos for free, exports MP4 and shares watermark-free. AI video generation is linked out to Adobe Firefly.

Check which assets are paid, and whether the hundredth video takes as long as the first.

Developer APIs for template video: Shotstack and Creatomate

Choose a video API when your developers want to build template video into your own software.

Shotstack

Shotstack's homepage calls it a cloud video editing API: design templates, including JSON templates, and render them through the API, with SDKs for Node.js, Python, PHP and Ruby and an embeddable white-label editor. It offers a free sandbox. See our Shotstack comparison.

Creatomate

Creatomate's homepage describes a video and image API for developers and no-code users: build a template in its online editor, then render it with data via REST, CSV, Zapier, Make or n8n. It offers a 50-credit trial. See our Creatomate comparison.

Check who maintains the templates and how failed renders are reported.

Many videos from your own pages and data: Videngine

Videngine fits when the same kind of video repeats across pages, listings, products or records you already have, and each must match its source.

It turns a web page, a listing, text or a row of data (URL, markdown, CSV or JSON) into a narrated, captioned MP4. AI writes the script and the voice; the pictures come from your source, placed by coded templates with your typefaces, colours and logo.

Each source typically gives 9:16 and 16:9 cuts up to 1080p, and every video is reviewed before it goes out. Videngine is coming soon: launch pricing starts at $79 a month for 50 videos, with no free plan.

When Videngine is not the answer

  • A shot that was never filmed: use Veo, Runway or Kling. Videngine does not generate footage.
  • A presenter on screen: use Synthesia or HeyGen. Videngine has no avatars.
  • Editing your own clips: use Canva, Adobe Express or InVideo. Videngine is not a timeline editor.
  • Stock footage for an article: use Pictory or Lumen5. Videngine has no stock library.
  • Video inside your own app: use Shotstack or Creatomate.
  • Narration in another language: Videngine narrates in English only.

How to choose an AI video maker

Choose by running the same brief through a short list, not by comparing feature lists.

  1. Name the job. Write down the input you have and the output you need to approve.
  2. Decide what must be true. If prices or specifications appear on screen, prefer tools that build from your source over tools that generate the picture.
  3. Write one test brief with a hard case, such as an awkward pronunciation or a portrait photo in a landscape frame.
  4. Run it through two or three tools from one section of this guide.
  5. Make one realistic correction. Change a price or replace a scene, and see what else moves.
  6. Inspect the export on the plan you would actually buy: resolution, captions, watermark, licence.
  7. Price the approved video with our AI video generator cost guide, then weigh named tools in our comparisons.

Questions about AI video generators

What is the best AI video generator in 2026?

There is no honest single winner, because the best AI video generators do different jobs. For generated footage, start with Google Veo, Runway or Kling; for presenters, Synthesia or HeyGen; for template video through an API, Shotstack or Creatomate. Pick the job first, then test two or three tools on the same brief.

What is the best free AI video generator?

Of the tools in this guide, Synthesia, HeyGen and Fliki state a free plan, Canva and Adobe Express offer free editors, and Shotstack and Creatomate offer a free sandbox or trial. Check the watermark, export resolution, allowance and commercial-use terms before relying on any of them.

Is OpenAI Sora still available?

According to OpenAI's help centre, the Sora web and app experiences were discontinued on 26 April 2026 and the Sora API was due to be discontinued on 24 September 2026. For generated footage, compare Google Veo, Runway and Kling instead.

What is the best AI video maker for a business?

It depends on what the video has to show. Presenter-led training suits Synthesia or HeyGen, and hand-built social posts suit Canva or Adobe Express. Videos that state prices or specifications across many records suit a template approach such as Shotstack, Creatomate or Videngine.

Is Videngine an AI video generator?

Not in the sense of generating footage. Videngine uses AI to write the script and record the narration, then renders the pictures from coded templates using your own page, listing or data. It is the wrong tool for a one-off cinematic clip or a presenter video.

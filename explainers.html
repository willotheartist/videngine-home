The short answer

Explainer video software comes in four kinds: avatar presenters, character animation, whiteboard drawing and motion graphics built from text. Pick by the job: characters and whiteboard suit concepts and classrooms, avatars suit training with a face, and motion graphics suit many explainers from documents you already keep. Videngine makes the last kind: narrated, captioned motion-graphics explainers from help articles, release notes and guides, many at a time.

Making one well

Whatever the style, a good explainer answers one question, in a script written for the ear, at a length that fits where it will be watched.

  1. Pick one question the viewer already has, such as "how do I export my invoices?"
  2. Write the script before choosing visuals: problem, answer, how it works, proof, next step. See how to write an explainer video script.
  3. Set the length by the job: shorter for ads and homepages, longer for how-to and training. See explainer video length and cost.
  4. Check the explanation is right, especially where AI drafted the script. See AI explainer video maker.
  5. Caption it, and put the transcript on the page where the video lives.

How Videngine makes explainers

When Videngine is the wrong tool

Videngine makes motion-graphics explainers only, so other styles belong in other tools.

  • Animated characters: an animation editor such as Powtoon or Animaker.
  • Whiteboard and doodle videos: whiteboard software such as VideoScribe; see the whiteboard guide.
  • A presenter on camera: an avatar platform, or a real person filmed.
  • Narration in other languages: Videngine narrates in English only.

Questions about explainer videos

What is the best explainer video software?

It depends on the style and the number of videos. Animation editors suit characters and presentations, whiteboard software suits a hand-drawn look, avatar platforms suit a presenter, and a programmatic engine such as Videngine suits many motion-graphics explainers made from documents.

How do I create an explainer video?

Pick one question the viewer has, write a script that answers it for the ear, choose a style, build the scenes, add narration and captions, and check the explanation is right before publishing. The guides on this page cover each step.

Can AI make an explainer video?

AI can draft the script, record a synthetic voice, write captions and plan scenes. A person still needs to make sure the explanation is correct, which is why every Videngine video is reviewed before it goes out.

Does Videngine make animated explainer videos?

It makes motion-graphics explainers: title cards, information cards, comparisons and narration over your own images. It does not make character animation, whiteboard videos or avatar presenters.

How long should an explainer video be?

It depends on the job. Ads and homepage videos are short, while how-to and training videos run longer; the explainer length and cost guide gives a starting length for each.

Training videos from your documents: an AI training video maker

How to turn the procedures, onboarding pages, lessons and leaflets you already maintain into training videos that stay current.

The short answer

A training video maker works best from documents a team already keeps accurate: procedures, onboarding pages, lesson notes and approved leaflets. Each document becomes a short narrated, captioned video with one objective, reviewed before release and re-rendered when the document changes. Videngine does this as MP4 files for an LMS, an intranet, YouTube or a website. It doesn't make avatar presenters, quizzes or SCORM packages, so for those an avatar training platform fits better.

What good training video needs

Good training video is short, has one objective, is captioned, and has been approved by the person responsible for the content.

  1. One objective per video, stated in the first sentence.
  2. Short: a few minutes rather than a recorded hour.
  3. Captioned, with a transcript, so it works with the sound off and for people who can't hear it.
  4. Approved by the owner of the procedure, course or leaflet before release.
  5. Versioned, so everyone knows which video matches which revision of the document.

Keeping videos current

When a procedure, policy or leaflet changes, the video should be re-rendered from the new version rather than patched.

Videngine saves a rebuild as a new version beside the original, so the ledger shows which video is current, and re-rendering the same video in the same month is free. A version number and date on the closing card ties the video to its document.

Delivery

Training videos are delivered as MP4 files, which most learning platforms, intranets and video hosts accept.

Videngine's files can be downloaded, put in a Google Drive folder you own, published to YouTube as unlisted or private, or published into a page of a static website with a transcript and caption track. If your learning platform needs SCORM packages, quizzes or completion tracking, those come from the platform or an avatar training tool, not from Videngine.

How Videngine makes training videos

Videngine turns written material into narrated, captioned videos, reviewed before release.

Paste a document's text, give it a page on your help centre or intranet by URL, or send rows of data. It writes a script you can accept, edit, or replace with your approved text word for word, builds scenes in your brand from title cards, information cards and your own screenshots and photos, records the narration in a house voice, another synthetic voice or your own recording, and captions it from the finished audio. A per-brand pronunciation lexicon keeps product names and terms right. Plans are priced per finished video: see pricing.

When Videngine is the wrong tool

Some training needs things Videngine doesn't do.

  • A presenter on screen, many languages, quizzes or SCORM: an avatar training platform; see Videngine vs Colossyan.
  • Hands-on physical tasks: film a real demonstration.
  • Live software click-throughs: a screen recorder.
  • Narration in other languages: Videngine narrates in English only.

Questions about training videos

What is the best AI training video generator?

It depends on what the training needs. Avatar platforms suit training that needs a presenter, many languages or quizzes; a document-to-video engine such as Videngine suits many short, narrated videos made from procedures, pages and notes you already maintain.

Can I turn a PDF or document into a training video?

Yes. Paste the document's text or give Videngine the page it lives on, and it writes a script you can edit or replace word for word, then renders a narrated, captioned video for review.

Can Videngine export SCORM?

No. Videngine delivers MP4 or WebM files, which most learning platforms accept as video; SCORM packages, quizzes and completion tracking come from the learning platform or an interactive training tool.

How do I keep training videos up to date?

Re-render the video from the revised document rather than editing the old file. Videngine keeps each rebuild as a new version, so you can see which video matches which revision.

Are training videos accessible without captions?

Captions are needed for people who can't hear the audio and for anyone watching with the sound off. Videngine captions every video from its finished audio, burned in or as a separate file.

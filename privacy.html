Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026

This policy explains how Wall & Fifth Ltd ("Wall & Fifth", "we", "us") collects and uses personal data in connection with Videngine at videngine.io and app.videngine.io (the "Service"). It applies to visitors to our website, people who join the launch waitlist, account holders, and people whose data appears in content our customers process through the Service.

1. Who we are

Wall & Fifth Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales, company number 16573579, registered office 20 Wenlock Road, London, N1 7GU. For most data described in this policy we are the data controller. Privacy questions: hello@videngine.io.

2. Controller or processor

  • Website, waitlist and account data (sections 3.1–3.4): we are the controller.
  • Customer Content (section 3.5): our customer is the controller and we act as its processor. We process Customer Content only on the customer's instructions under our Terms of Service. If your data appears in a video or listing created by one of our customers, contact that customer first; we will help them respond.

3. What we collect

3.1 Launch waitlist. The email address you submit, the date you joined, the source of the signup, a record of your consent to receive a launch notification, and whether that notification has been sent. Joining does not create an account or start a subscription.

3.2 Account data. Name, work email, organisation, role, password (hashed) and account settings.

3.3 Usage and technical data. IP address, browser and device type, pages and features used, render activity, timestamps, error logs and approximate location derived from IP.

3.4 Communications. Emails, support messages and enquiries you send us, and our replies.

3.5 Customer Content. Listings, product data, images, footage, audio, logos, text, feeds and the videos rendered from them. This may contain personal data such as contact names, phone numbers, addresses, or people visible in footage. What it contains is determined by the customer.

3.6 Billing data. Billing contact, invoicing address and payment records. Card details, where taken, are handled by our payment provider and not stored by us.

We do not knowingly collect special category data and ask customers not to upload it.

9. Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit, hashed passwords, private repositories and infrastructure, and least-privilege access to systems. No system is completely secure; if we become aware of a personal data breach affecting you, we will notify you and, where required, the ICO without undue delay.

10. Your rights

Under UK GDPR and, where it applies, EU GDPR, you can ask us to: access the personal data we hold about you; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to how we use it; provide it in a portable format; and withdraw consent where consent is the basis. You can opt out of emails using the link in any message or by contacting us.

To exercise a right, email hello@videngine.io. We will respond within one month and may ask you to verify your identity. If your data is in Customer Content, we will pass your request to the relevant customer as controller.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or to your local supervisory authority if you are in the EEA. We would appreciate the chance to resolve any concern first.

11. Cookies

videngine.io sets no cookies. Our analytics run in memory only, without cookies or cross-site tracking, and are hosted in the EU. app.videngine.io uses session cookies needed to keep you signed in; it will not work without them. We do not use advertising cookies.

12. Children

The Service is for business users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version with a new "last updated" date and notify account holders of material changes by email or in the Service.

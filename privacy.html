Launch waitlist privacy

Updated 21 September 2026

What you are signing up for

Videngine, operated by Wall & Fifth, stores the email address you submit so we can notify you when Videngine launches. Joining does not create an account or start a paid subscription.

What we store

Your email address, the date you joined, the source of the signup and a record of your agreement to receive a launch notification. We also record whether a launch notification has been sent, to help avoid repeat messages.

How we use it

We use your email for the launch notification you requested. We do not sell the waitlist or use it to sign you up to unrelated marketing. Our hosting providers process this information to operate the service. The waitlist is not publicly accessible.

Your choice

You can withdraw your consent or request access, correction or deletion by emailing hello@videngine.io. We keep your waitlist entry until the launch notification has been sent, or until you ask us to delete it. After launch we will remove entries that are no longer needed for that purpose.

YouTube Shorts vs long-form videos: which a business needs

YouTube Shorts vs long videos: most businesses need both, cut from the same listing, product or article. What each is for, where to start, and a workflow.

The short answer

YouTube Shorts vs long videos is rarely an either-or for a business: most need both, made from the same listing, product or article. A Short is a vertical or square video of up to three minutes, met in the Shorts feed by people who were not looking. A long-form video is wider, usually 16:9, and is found by people searching for the exact thing. Videngine renders both cuts from one source, each with its own title.

YouTube Shorts vs long videos: what actually differs

Shape and length decide which is which, not the subject. For uploads from 15 October 2024, YouTube categorises a square or vertical video of up to three minutes as a Short, and its help page on three-minute Shorts says to use a wider aspect ratio, such as 16:9, for long-form.

So long-form does not have to mean long. A 90-second landscape video of one product is a long-form YouTube video, found and watched differently from its 30-second vertical cut.

How one listing, product or article becomes two cuts

One source gives one set of facts and photos; the two cuts differ in what opens, how much they carry and how they end.

Take an illustrative product page for a two-person tent, with six landscape photos, a weight, a packed size, floor dimensions and a price:

ElementShort (9:16)Landscape (16:9)
OpeningThe best photo, with the weight on screenProduct name and who it is for
FactsWeight and packed sizeWeight, packed size, floor dimensions, materials and what is in the bag
PhotosThree, each framed over a blurred fill of itself so nothing is cropped outAll six, one scene each
Length25 to 40 seconds60 to 120 seconds
EndingA held final frame pointing to the product pagePrice and where to buy
TitleThis two-person tent weighs 1.4 kgTwo-person backpacking tent, 1.4 kg: specification and packed size

The same split works for other sources. For a listing, the Short opens on the fact buyers filter on, such as price, year or location, and the landscape cut walks through every photo. For an article, the Short carries the single finding; the landscape cut uses chapter title cards and information cards for the rest.

Keep every claim in both cuts to what the source says: if the page gives no pitch time, neither cut should. Short-form video at volume and YouTube videos made from your pages cover each side in more depth.

Which to start with, by business type

Start with the format that matches how your buyers look: if they search for the exact thing, start long-form; if they come across it while browsing, start with Shorts.

Monetisation: Shorts and long-form count separately

Most business channels exist to sell the listing or product, not to earn from ads; if ad revenue matters too, YouTube counts the two formats separately.

The YouTube Partner Program eligibility page gives two routes: 1,000 subscribers with 4,000 qualified public watch hours in the last 12 months, or 1,000 subscribers with 10 million qualified public Shorts views in the last 90 days. Watch hours from Shorts in the Shorts Feed do not count towards the 4,000 hours.

The three-minute Shorts page has one rule that affects music: a Short longer than one minute with an active copyright claim is blocked globally, so use only music you are cleared to use. For the policies on synthetic and repetitive content, see YouTube's rules on AI and repetitive content.

A workflow for both cuts from the same source

Treat the pair as one job: one source, one review, two uploads.

  1. Pick the sources. Pages from your sitemap, rows of a CSV (see bulk video creation from a CSV) or a JSON payload.
  2. Set both formats once. 9:16 and 16:9 at 1080p, captions burned in, brand settings, and a target length in the voice rules.
  3. Choose the hook and the detail. Decide which fact opens the Short and what only the landscape cut carries. Accept the scene plan, edit a line, or supply your own script.
  4. Pilot five to ten sources. Watch both cuts of each. Fix problems in the template, voice rules or data, not in single videos.
  5. Check the metadata against the source. Two titles, two angles, no claim the page does not make.
  6. Publish unlisted first. Check the first batch on YouTube, then switch the default to public.
  7. Link the Short to its long cut. In YouTube Studio, add the landscape video as the Short's related video; viewers see a clickable link below your channel handle. It needs advanced feature access.
  8. Retire or rebuild when the source changes. When an item sells or a price changes, make both cuts private or rebuild them; a rebuild is saved as a new version beside the original.

When to make only one, or use another tool

Make one cut when the second would add nothing; use another tool when the video depends on a person, a recording or a trend.

  • Shorts only: stock that sells within days, which nobody will search for once it has gone, and one-fact updates such as a new arrival or a price change.
  • Long-form only: how-to videos whose steps or screenshots are unreadable at phone width, side-by-side comparisons, and videos you mainly embed on your own page.
  • Not from Videngine: talking to camera (film it, or compare avatar presenter tools such as Synthesia); highlights from podcasts or webinars (a clipping tool, since Videngine does not edit recordings); trends and lip-sync (a phone and an editing app).

Questions about Shorts and long-form

Should I make Shorts or long-form videos for my business?

Make both if you have a catalogue of listings, products or articles: Shorts reach people browsing the Shorts feed, and long-form videos reach people searching for the exact thing. If you can only make one, match how your buyers look: searchers point to long-form, browsers to Shorts.

Is a two-minute video a Short or a long-form video?

It depends on the shape, not only the length. For uploads from 15 October 2024, YouTube categorises a square or vertical video of up to three minutes as a Short. A two-minute video in 16:9 is long-form.

Can I link a YouTube Short to my long-form video?

Yes. In YouTube Studio you can add a related video from your own channel to a Short, and viewers see a clickable link below your channel handle. The linked video must be public or unlisted, and the feature needs advanced feature access.

Are YouTube Shorts for business worth making?

They are worth it when they cost little beyond the long-form video: the same source, photos and review, with a different opening and title. Judge them by whether viewers go on to the listing, product or long cut, not by views alone.

Do Shorts views count towards YouTube monetisation?

Yes, through their own route to the YouTube Partner Program: 1,000 subscribers and 10 million qualified public Shorts views in the last 90 days. Watch hours from Shorts in the Shorts Feed do not count towards the 4,000 watch hours route.

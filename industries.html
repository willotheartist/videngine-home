Automated video by industry: dealers, brokers, marketplaces and more

Which industries suit automated video, what each one turns into a video, and the guide written for it.

The short answer

Automated video suits industries whose work produces many similar things worth a video: vehicles and boats for sale, properties, products, software releases, articles and service pages. Videngine sets each workspace up for its industry, so a dealer imports listings, a shop imports products and a publisher imports articles, with templates and review to match. The pictures always come from the business's own listings, pages and images, never from generated footage.

Listing businesses

Listing businesses gain the most from automated video because every item already has photos, a specification and a reference number, and the stock turns over faster than anyone can film it.

A car dealer needs a walkaround in the right photo order, the mileage and specification read correctly and the video retired when the car sells. A yacht broker needs builder and marina names pronounced properly and a separate template for charter. An estate or lettings agent needs room-by-room order and a fast re-render when the price or status changes. A marketplace needs all of that across thousands of sellers, with quality gates, seller opt-outs and delivery straight into the listings.

What they share: files named by stock number, a vertical cut for social and a landscape cut for the website and YouTube, and a ledger that shows which version is current.

Product and catalogue businesses

Shops and brands use automated video to give every product, not just the best sellers, a video built from the catalogue they already keep accurate.

The hard parts are variants and offers: a video must show the colour and size it names, and a price in a video needs an owner who replaces it when the price changes. Product video automation covers the record structure and refresh rules; the video ads guide covers turning the same catalogue into ad variants.

Software, media and services

For businesses whose product is a service, software or writing, the source is a page or a document rather than a listing, and the video keeps pace with what they publish.

Software companies turn release notes, feature pages and help articles into short clips, re-rendered when the interface changes. Publishers turn evergreen articles into videos and Shorts, with image rights cleared and the desk signing off. Service businesses, from agencies to clinics to installers, usually start with their service pages: URL to video for one page, then a YouTube channel or ads built from the rest.

What stays the same

Whatever the industry, Videngine reads the source, writes a script you can edit, builds scenes from coded templates in your brand, narrates with your pronunciation rules, captions from the finished audio and renders 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 4:5 MP4s up to 1080p.

  1. Every video is reviewed in the studio before it goes out, and a rejected video carries a defect code such as pronunciation, specification or photo.
  2. A rebuild is saved as a new version beside the original, so the ledger always shows which file is current.
  3. Finished videos are downloaded, delivered to a Google Drive folder you own or published to a connected YouTube channel; Custom plans can deliver straight into your listings.

Questions about video by industry

Which industries is Videngine built for?

Businesses with many similar things worth a video: vehicle and equipment dealers, boat brokers, estate and lettings agents, marketplaces, online shops, software companies, publishers and service businesses. Each workspace is set up for its industry, so the studio's wording and templates follow what the business sells.

Does automated video work for a small dealer or broker?

Yes, if there is enough stock to make a template worthwhile. Launch pricing starts at $79 a month for 50 finished videos, which covers a small forecourt or a broker's current listings, and same-month re-renders after a price change are free.

Can one company run several brands?

Yes. Brands are included in each plan: one on Starter, three on Growth and ten on Scale, each with its own type, colours, logo and voice rules. Larger groups agree a Custom package.

Do we need to film anything?

No. Videngine builds videos from the photos, screenshots and images you already have in your listings, product pages or articles. Filmed footage can be added where you have it, but most listing and product videos need none.
